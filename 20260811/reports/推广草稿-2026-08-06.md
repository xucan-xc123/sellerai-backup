### The 2026 Amazon Listing Writing Framework

**Step 1 — Keyword Research With Intent Mapping**

Modern Amazon SEO depends on matching search intent, not just search volume. Extract seed keywords, expand with autocomplete and competitor analysis, group by intent (commercial vs. informational), and distribute terms accordingly.

**Step 2 — Title Architecture for Both A9 and Rufus**

Place your primary keyword within the first 80 characters, follow with the benefit, and add key specs toward the end. A strong example: *"Wireless Earbuds for Running, IPX5 Waterproof Bluetooth 5.3 Sport Headphones with 40H Battery"* — this answers both the search query and the Rufus question.

**Step 3 — Bullet Points That Close**

Structure each bullet as: customer benefit + feature that delivers it + proof point. Vary intent across your five bullets: one for the primary use case, one for build quality, one for trust signals, one for comparison advantage, and one for what's included.

> Weak: "40-hour battery life for long listening sessions."
> Strong: "Run a full marathon week without recharging — 40-hour battery life means your playlist outlasts your legs."

**Step 4 — Product Description Optimization**

On mobile, only the first 2–3 lines are visible. Front-load your strongest claim. Open with a hook that agitates the shopper's pain point, follow with the solution, embed 3–5 long-tail keywords naturally, and close with a trust signal.

**Step 5 — Backend Search Terms**

Use them for synonyms, common misspellings, alternate product names, and language equivalents. Never repeat words already in your title or bullets.

**Step 6 — Measure, Feed Back, Iterate**

Amazon SEO is a loop. Every two weeks, feed winning search terms from your Search Term Report back into your listing and update backend terms with new long-tail queries.
