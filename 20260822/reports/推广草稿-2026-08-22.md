**标题:** The 25-Point Amazon Listing Audit That Actually Moves Your Rank in 2026

**正文:**

Most Amazon sellers don't have a product problem — they have a listing process problem. They write the title once, paste the bullets from a supplier sheet, and never touch the page again. In 2026, that's expensive.

This is the checklist I use with clients to audit any listing in about twenty minutes. No folklore, no guesswork — just 25 points ranked by impact.

**The Title (Points 1-5)**

- Lead with your primary keyword in the first 80 characters. Amazon truncates mobile titles, and whatever comes after character 80 gets lost.
- Put product type before brand name. New sellers with low brand search volume should not lead with the brand.
- Include one differentiator (size, count, material) your competitors' titles skip.
- Stay under 200 characters. Longer titles dilute keyword weight.
- No ALL CAPS, no promo language ("free shipping," "best price") — both violate Amazon's listing quality guidelines.

**The Bullets (Points 6-10)**

- Front-load every bullet with a benefit, not a feature. "Stays silent while you sleep" beats "Rubber feet included."
- Use a consistent structure: benefit, proof (spec or number), then a micro-detail that builds trust.
- Answer the five buyer objections: durability, ease of use, compatibility, size/fit, and value.
- Sprinkle 1-2 long-tail keywords per bullet naturally. Don't stuff — Amazon's systems punish keyword soup.
- Keep bullets scannable: no paragraphs over three lines, em-dashes and commas for rhythm, never emoji spam.

**Backend Keywords (Points 11-15)**

- Use all 250 bytes. Unused space is wasted ranking surface.
- Include misspellings and regional synonyms shoppers actually type ("colour," "favourite," "organiser").
- Never repeat words already in your title or bullets.
- Never include competitor brand names, ASINs, or trademarks.
- Revisit quarterly: drop low-impression phrases, add new long-tails from your search query report.

**Images and A+ Content (Points 16-20)**

- Main image: pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), product fills at least 85% of the frame.
- First three images decide conversion. Mobile shoppers rarely swipe past three.
- Add a size/dimension graphic — size confusion is the #1 return driver on Amazon.
- A+ content: comparison modules showing why your variant wins convert measurably better.
- Keep images under 1MB. Slow images hurt mobile conversion.

**Rufus and AI Visibility (Points 21-25)**

- Write the way shoppers ask questions. Rufus reads review language, listing copy, and Q&A.
- Match review vocabulary — pull exact buyer phrases and work them into bullets and backend.
- Keep your Q&A section active and answered in natural language.
- Structure copy with clear topic sentences. AI and human scanners both reward structure.
- Audit monthly: check Search Query Performance for phrases you rank for but your listing doesn't mention.

**Run It in 20 Minutes**

Open your worst-performing listing with traffic. Score it against all 25 points. If it scores under 15, the listing is your bottleneck — not your product, not your ads. Fix title and bullets first (points 1-10 are highest leverage), then images, then backend. Most sellers see movement in search impressions within two to three weeks after re-indexing.
